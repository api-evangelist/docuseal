Explains the three main types of electronic signatures under EU eIDAS regulation: Simple Electronic Signature (SES) for everyday transactions, Advanced Electronic Signature (AES) for secure transactions requiring identity verification, and Qualified Electronic Signature (QES) for regulated transactions requiring the highest security through certified authorities.
