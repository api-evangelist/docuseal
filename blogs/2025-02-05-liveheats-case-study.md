Liveheats, an event registration company, overcame document signing bottlenecks by switching to DocuSeal's API, which handles traffic spikes better than their previous provider and eliminated signing-related support tickets entirely. Co-founder and CEO Chris Friend reported going from unhappy customers and heavy support load to zero signing-related support.
